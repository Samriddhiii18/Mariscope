What to do next

A practical response, not just a red flag

1
Check authorization

Compare vessel identity, flag, licence and local zone rules.

2
Confirm the behaviour

HOW IT WORKS

Simple on the surface. Serious underneath.

The same five steps power every mission.

1

Observe

Satellite radar sees the sea. AIS and approved vessel feeds show what ships report. GNSS-R adds a developing passive-radar view.

→
2

Compare

Put every signal in the same time and place. Match an observed vessel with the broadcast it should have.

→
3

Understand

Add zone rules, wind, waves, coastlines, repeat sightings and fishing or spill context.

→
4

Prioritise

Score evidence and uncertainty. Show high, review and uncertain cases instead of one confusing red flag.

→
5

Act

Recommend the next check: re-observe, verify authorisation, dispatch a response asset or document the case.

i

What the system will never claim by itself

A missing AIS signal is not proof of illegal behaviour. A dark radar feature is not proof of oil. Mariscope is a decision-support layer: the relevant authority confirms the event and decides what action is lawful.
